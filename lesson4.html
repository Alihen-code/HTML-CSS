french fries Recipe

There's nothing quite like a batch of hot, crispy homemade French fries fresh from the fryer. Golden on the outside and soft on the inside, these fries are the perfect snack or side dish for any meal. With a few simple ingredients and easy-to-follow steps, you can make restaurant-quality French fries right in your own kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 4 Large Potatos
  • cold water
  • cooking oil for frying
  • 1-2 tablespoon salt

Instructions

  1. Wash and peel the potatoes (optional). Cut them into thin sticks of equal size. Place the potato sticks in a bowl of cold water for at least 30 minutes (or up to several hours). This helps remove excess starch and makes them crispier.
  2. Drain the water and pat the potatoes completely dry with paper towels.
  3. Heat oil to about 160°C (320°F). Fry the potatoes in small batches for 3–5 minutes. They should soften but not turn brown. Remove and let them rest for 5–10 minutes.
  4. Increase the oil temperature to 190°C (375°F). Fry the potatoes again for 2–4 minutes until golden brown and crispy. Remove and drain on paper towels
  5. Sprinkle with salt while they're still hot. Serve immediately
