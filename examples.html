6 Interactive Elements

Disclosure widgets, modal dialogs, and templates — all native, no libraries.

Details & summary
What is a <details> element?

A native disclosure widget. The <summary> is the always-visible label; everything after it is revealed when the widget is expanded. No JavaScript required.

Why use semantic HTML?

Screen readers, search engines, and reader modes all rely on structure. Using the right element for the job gives you accessibility and SEO for free.

Is the <template> element rendered?

No — its contents are inert until cloned by JavaScript. Below, a template is stamped into the page three times.
